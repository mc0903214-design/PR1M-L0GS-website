Recommendations
Follow the recommendations for working with accounts to ensure security and efficiency.

Use high-quality proxy servers.


Problem:

If you log in to multiple accounts from the same IP address, all your accounts can be blocked.


Solution:

Use quality proxy servers, such as individual IPv4 proxies or Resident proxies. Avoid package proxies and browser extensions such as Hola, FreeVPN, etc. IPv6 proxies are also not recommended.

Use different devices to log in to different accounts.


Problem:

If you log in to multiple accounts from one device, all accounts can be blocked.


Solution:

Use different devices or specialized software for each account. Incognito mode, clearing cookies, or opening another browser tab does not count as a different device.

  • UserAgent changes
  • Anti-detect browsers

Solution:
